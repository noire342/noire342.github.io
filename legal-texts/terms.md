# Nyanime Terms and Licence Scope

Review draft — 11 October 2026. This website offers no application download or approved proprietary release. These pages describe the project and proposed licence boundaries; they do not impose new terms on copies already received.

## The project

Nyanime is an Android library, reader and media-player project developed by Lorenzo Garrone. The planned public base app has no preinstalled content sources, addons or developer-managed source catalogue. It does not grant rights to third-party media. Use only files, sources and services you are entitled to access.

## Original work and shared foundations

Nyanime preserves work from AniYomi, Mihon, Tachiyomi and other contributors. Their code and third-party components retain their applicable licences. Root Apache License 2.0 and inherited notices remain available. Previous grants are not revoked by private development or different plans for later releases.

Proprietary terms are proposed only for original contributions whose ownership and scope are established in a release-specific schedule. That schedule is incomplete. This does not label the whole app, inherited files or existing contributions proprietary. Read the [proposed original-contribution licence](https://noire342.github.io/license-texts/LICENSE-NYANIME-DRAFT.txt) and [licence notices](https://noire342.github.io/nyanime/licenses/).

## Open-source rights

Open-source licences take precedence for their components. The proposed restrictions preserve LGPL modification, replacement, relinking/recomposition and reverse engineering to debug those modifications, MPL source rights and other third-party permissions. A release must deliver the sources, notices and practical replacement materials required by its licences. An EULA alone does not make an incomplete distribution compliant.

## Sources and external accounts

An imported `.nyasource` requires explicit approval. Its signature verifies its signing key and integrity, not content copyright or host authorization. Independent modules and services apply their own terms. Nyanime is not affiliated with a host simply because a user configures access.

Tracker access requires an available integration and a valid application registration where required. Telegram, room relays and Cast receivers are external services/devices. Keep credentials, room invitations and recovery authority private.

## Privacy and backups

Read the [Privacy Policy](https://noire342.github.io/nyanime/privacy/) before using network features. Local exports may include unencrypted credentials. This candidate requires application-layer encryption for new Telegram uploads; old plaintext backups remain readable for restore compatibility. Clearing local data does not erase independent copies or revoke credentials. See [Manage data](https://noire342.github.io/nyanime/data/).

## Release status and statutory rights

Dependency, ownership, source-delivery and recomposition checks remain in progress. Store acceptance, continuous external-service availability and an approved proprietary release are not promised. A future executable licence must identify its release and preserve mandatory consumer, interoperability and other statutory rights.
