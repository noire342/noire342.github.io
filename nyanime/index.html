The project today

Designed around your device.

Library entries, progress, preferences and downloads are managed locally. Network features use the external services you select or the services needed for their functions.

The planned public base app has no preinstalled content sources or developer-managed source catalogue. User-approved portable sources and optional cloud features are being reviewed as part of the development candidate.

No application download is offered here. Ownership, dependency and release checks remain in progress. Google Play availability has not been confirmed.

Project features

More time with your favourites.

These features are present in the project. The development candidate and its final release requirements are still being reviewed.

01

Library & progress

Pick up where you left off.

Organize your personal library and keep viewing, reading and resume progress together on your device.

02

Manga & local files

Read at your pace.

A manga reader with local-file support and configurable reading controls, alongside your library.

03

Player & Anime4K

A closer look.

Video playback with track and subtitle controls, plus configurable Anime4K shader processing. Results depend on the media and device.

04

Agenda & notifications

Keep your plans nearby.

Organize upcoming viewing and reading with the project's agenda and notification controls. External schedule information depends on available integrations.

05

Watch & read together

Share the moment.

Coordinate playback or reading in invitation-based rooms. Encrypted room payloads use external relays, which can still observe connection and event metadata.

06

Backups

Keep a copy of your place.

Export portable .nyabk backups of library data and selected settings. Media files are excluded; local exports may include unencrypted credentials.

07

Interface & accessibility

Make it comfortable.

An Android interface with adjustable reader, playback and display preferences. Accessibility and final-device validation remain part of release review.
