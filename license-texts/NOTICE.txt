Nyanime — experimental closed-source-player branch

Nyanime derives from AniYomi and preserves work from Mihon, Tachiyomi and their
contributors. The upstream Apache License 2.0 and original notices remain
applicable to their respective code. This notice does not replace any licence.

Modifications in this branch include removal of the torrent engine and optional
addon host, additional component notices, a portable interpreted source runtime,
and experimental native-player build and verification recipes.
Runtime dependency inventories and corrected component notices distinguish the
licences of Java wrappers from the native libraries linked into the application.

Native media libraries, image decoders, shaders, SDKs, fonts, models and other
assets retain their individual licences. Source availability, replacement and
recomposition obligations apply where required. Current default player binaries
are not replaced merely by adding this notice or by preparing new native AARs.

See docs/licensing.md, docs/credits.md and docs/closed-source-player.md for the
component boundaries, verification status and remaining distribution requirements.
No statement of Google Play approval or closed-source release readiness is made.
