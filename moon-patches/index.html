A few steps to your first room

Make room
for Moon.

Moon Patches is a patch bundle, rather than a standalone app. You apply it with Morphe.

Add the source.

In Morphe, open Sources → + → Remote and paste this address. Or use Add to Morphe on Android.

https://github.com/noire342/moon-patches

  1. 01

    Choose Moon.

    Select Moon Patches as your YouTube source. Its bundle includes the base patches: avoid selecting equivalent patches from another source.

  2. 02

    Apply your patches.

    Use a compatible original YouTube APK. Include Nya Rooms, Custom branding and Change header. Patch and install.

  3. 03

    Meet in a room.

    Select Moon in the branding settings. Find the rooms button beside Search or in the player. Create a room, share the code and approve your friend.

Before your first shared moment

Good to know.

What can we watch together?⌄

Rooms support regular YouTube videos, with up to eight participants and six-hour sessions. Shorts, live streams and casting are outside the supported room modes. Chat, playlist sync, host transfer and Nyanime room interoperability are not available yet.

The integration is experimental. Check the validation record for tested versions and remaining coverage.

Do rooms send the video through Nostr?⌄

No. Each device plays its own YouTube stream. Nostr relays carry encrypted room coordination, rather than video; network conditions and buffering can differ.

How private is a room?⌄

Session identities and keys are temporary. Approved members can read room messages; public relays can observe connection metadata, including IP addresses. Host approval and fingerprint checks help establish trust. This is not an anonymous or confidential chat service.

Moon adds no analytics or account backend. Read the privacy details for retention and optional services.

What happens to SponsorBlock?⌄

SponsorBlock is suspended during room creation, joining, reconnecting and participation to keep shared playback coordinated. Your settings are retained.
